    Limits used: max wind resistance 10.7 m/s (Level 5), operating temperature −10 to 40 °C, Normal-mode top speed 8 m/s, GPS + Galileo + BeiDou. The Avata 2 has no water resistance. Thresholds live in one LIMITS object if you want to tune them.
